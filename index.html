Galaxy Defender AR

Five missions in your own room. Holographic ships pour out of portals you pin to the walls, along with asteroids, mines and bosses. Shoot with one hand, block with a shield in the other, and dodge with your whole body.

Checking whether this device supports AR…
ShootPull the trigger, or pinch with your shooting hand. On desktop: hold the mouse button.
ShieldRaise your free hand in front of you, or hold the controller's bumper. On desktop: Shift.
DodgeLean or step aside. On desktop: WASD, C to crouch.
PauseThe “II” button on the score panel, or P. The game also pauses when you leave the app.
Based on Galaxy Defender, a university project built in Unity 2019.2.
